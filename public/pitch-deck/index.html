05 / The Core Trust Engine

Solving the "Trust Deficit"

  • Pre-Paid Reservations: Payment is processed upfront at booking, guaranteeing confirmed orders for vendors and reserved portions for consumers.
  • Secure OTP Verification: A unique 4-digit pickup code is verified at collection, triggering immediate automatic vendor settlement.
  • Automated Strike System: Standardized vendor rules. Auto-suspended after 3 rolling strikes in a month.
  • No-Show Forfeits: Payout is automatically split if a customer fails to pick up their bag during the window.

02 / The Crisis

The Cost of Organic Landfill Waste

  • Potent Greenhouse Gases: Decomposing organic food in landfills is a primary driver of urban methane (CH₄) emissions.
  • Resource Squandering: Food losses throw away the land, clean water, and fuel used during farm production and cooking.
  • Access Gaps: Tons of cooked meals are thrown away daily while low-income families cut back on necessary portions.
